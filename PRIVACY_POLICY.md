# Nexus Life Privacy Policy

**Developed by:** Kuldeep Kumar Yadav  
**Project name:** Kuldeep Digital Services

## Information stored on your device

Tasks, habits, reminders, preferences and related app content are stored locally on your device. Nexus Life does not require an account. If you export a backup, the file is saved or shared through the destination you select.

## Notifications, voice announcements and Charging Focus

If enabled, reminders and Charging Focus prompts are scheduled by Android on your device. Voice Announcements use the text-to-speech service installed on your phone and do not require microphone access. If you create a Charging Focus emergency PIN, the app stores a salted password hash on your device; it does not upload the PIN. Speech processing is performed by the configured speech service.

## Google Play version analytics

The Google Play version uses PostHog to record app usage events, app version, event time, subscription status and event-specific settings or action details to understand usage and improve the app. The FOSS version does not send these analytics events. PostHog may process technical information under its own privacy policy.

## Subscriptions and payments

Where subscriptions are available in the Google Play version, Google Play and RevenueCat are used to process purchases and verify subscription access. Nexus Life does not store payment card or bank details.

## Sharing and service providers

Nexus Life does not sell personal information. The Play version's analytics and subscription features may send relevant event or purchase information to the providers named above. Android and your installed text-to-speech provider process information needed to provide their system services.

## Your choices

You can disable reminders, voice announcements and Charging Focus in Settings. You can choose the FOSS distribution, avoid using analytics-enabled Play features, and decide whether to export or share backups. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data unless your device separately backs it up.

## Sensitive information and children

Do not enter passwords, payment details or other highly sensitive information into task or habit descriptions. Nexus Life is a general productivity app and is not designed to collect sensitive personal information.

## Changes and contact

This policy may change when app features or service providers change. For questions, use the contact or issue options on the [official project page](https://github.com/Kuldeep532/Grit).
