Nexus Life brings daily planning and habit building together in one simple, private workspace.

Plan what matters today, build routines at your own pace and understand your progress over time. Your information stays on your device, and you can use the app without creating an account.

TASKS AND DAILY PLANNING
• Organise tasks with your own categories
• Break larger tasks into smaller steps
• Set reminders for important tasks
• Reorder tasks and mark them complete
• Keep completed work easy to review

HABIT TRACKING
• Create simple or measurable habits
• Choose the days each habit should appear
• Set reminders for routines
• Follow current and best streaks
• Review consistency over time

PROGRESS AND INSIGHTS
• View habit calendars and activity heat maps
• Compare weekly progress
• Explore monthly and overall summaries
• Understand which days work best for your routines

USEFUL HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
• See your tasks at a glance
• Check your habit overview
• Follow streaks and weekly activity

MADE TO FIT YOUR ROUTINE
• Light and dark appearance
• Custom colours and fonts
• Biometric app lock
• Flexible week-start and clock settings
• Layouts for phones, tablets and foldables

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
• No account required
• Works offline for everyday use
• Export and restore your information
• No advertisements
• No subscription or locked features in this edition
• Open source under the GNU GPL-3.0 licence

Nexus Life is built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. The project is based on the open-source Grit project and retains its GPL-3.0 licensing and required attribution.
